# Date: April 2026

## Two structures, one shared constant

### Structure 1: Number field mass lattice
- Field: Q(sqrt(5)), unit phi, spacing log(phi)
- Vacuum: empty lattice (no q selected)
- Particle: integer q, mass = me * phi^(q/4)
- Empirical anchor: me = 0.511 MeV (not derived)

### Structure 2: Hyperbolic geodesic spectrum
- Manifolds: m003 (lepton), m006 (quark)
- Vacuum: bare M — descriptors vol(M), CS(M), H1(M)=Z/5, sys(M)
- Particle: closed geodesic gamma — ell, phi, n_hat, [gamma] in Z/5
- ell(gamma) -> mass coordinate
- phi(gamma) -> CP phase
- n_hat(gamma) -> flavor mixing axis on S^2
- [gamma] in Z/5 -> generation label

### Shared constant
log(phi) = Reg(Q(sqrt(5))) = (1/2)*log M(Alex(m003 cusped))
         ~ l2(m006) = 0.4906  (0.5%)
         ~ sigma_CKM = 0.488  (1.4%)

### The open gap
- me = 0.511 MeV is not derived from the geometry
- sys(m003)/log(phi) = 1.2013 (not integer)
- sys(m006)/log(phi) = 0.7816 (not integer)
- The two structures share log(phi) but are not unified
- Unification would require: one geometric object whose
  geodesic spectrum gives mass lattice AND mixing matrices
