A Parameter-Free Prediction of the Leptonic CP Phase}

\author[1]{Marvin L. Gentry}

\begin{abstract}
We derive the leptonic CP-violating phase parameter-freely from the
holonomy geometry of the compact hyperbolic 3-manifold
$\MPMNS = m003(-2,3)$ (\texttt{OrientableClosedCensus[1]},
volume $0.9814$, $H_1=\Zfive$).
The twist angles $\varphi(\gamma)=\mathrm{Im}\log\lambda(\gamma)$
of two loxodromic holonomy words yield:
\[
  \delta_{\mathrm{HFG}}
  = \bigl(\pi + \varphi(\mathrm{aaB}) + \varphi(\mathrm{baa})\bigr)
    \bmod 360^\circ
  = 195.91^\circ,
\]
within $1.09^\circ$ of the PDG~2024 best fit $197.0^\circ$
($0.55\%$ error, zero free parameters).
The sign of $\pi$ is fixed geometrically by the orientation of the
axis triple: $\det[\hat{n}(\mathrm{aa}),\hat{n}(\mathrm{aaB}),
\hat{n}(\mathrm{baa})]=+0.079>0$.
The same manifold's trace field $\KPMNS=\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$
(imaginary quadratic) provides a geometric explanation for why the
lepton sector has large CP violation while the quark sector
(trace field $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{17})$, real) is geometrically CP-suppressed.
The prediction is falsifiable by Hyper-Kamiokande and DUNE.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

The leptonic CP-violating phase $\delta_{\mathrm{PMNS}}$ is the least
well-measured parameter of the Standard Model flavor sector.
The PDG~2024 best fit is $\delta=197.0^\circ$ with a $1\sigma$
uncertainty of approximately $\pm23^\circ$; Hyper-Kamiokande and
DUNE are expected to reduce this to $\pm5^\circ$ precision.
No first-principles derivation of $\delta$ exists.

In this paper we show that $\delta$ is encoded in the twist angles
of the holonomy representation of a single compact hyperbolic
3-manifold, $\MPMNS=m003(-2,3)$, with zero free parameters.
The result is part of the Hyperbolic Flavor Geometry (HFG)
framework~\cite{Gentry:Unified}, in which the PMNS mixing matrix
and charged lepton masses are also derived from the geometry of
$\MPMNS$.

All computations use SnapPy~\cite{SnapPy} at 150-bit precision.

\section{The Manifold and Its Holonomy}
\label{sec:manifold}

The manifold $\MPMNS=m003(-2,3)$ has volume $0.9814$,
$H_1=\Zfive$, and Chern-Simons invariant $1/4$.
Its invariant trace field is $\KPMNS=\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$
(imaginary quadratic)~\cite{Chinburg98}, with ring of integers
$\mathbb{Z}[\omega]$, $\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}$.

The holonomy representation $\rho:\pi_1(\MPMNS)\to\PSL$ is
computed via SnapPy's polished holonomy at 150-bit precision.
For a loxodromic element $\gamma\in\pi_1(\MPMNS)$, the dominant
eigenvalue $\lambda(\gamma)$ of $\rho(\gamma)$ satisfies
$|\lambda(\gamma)|>1$, and the \emph{twist angle} is
$\varphi(\gamma)=\mathrm{Im}\log\lambda(\gamma)\in(-\pi,\pi]$.

The axis direction $\hat{n}(\gamma)\in S^2$ is extracted from
the Pauli decomposition of $\log\rho(\gamma)$:
\[
  \log\rho(\gamma)
  = \tfrac{\eta}{2}I
  + \tfrac{i}{2}(n_x\sigma_x+n_y\sigma_y+n_z\sigma_z),
  \quad
  \hat{n}=\frac{(n_x,n_y,n_z)}{\|(n_x,n_y,n_z)\|},
\]
where $\eta=\ell(\gamma)+i\varphi(\gamma)$ is the complex
translation length.

\section{The CP Phase Formula}
\label{sec:formula}

\begin{theorem}[Leptonic CP phase, zero free parameters]
\label{thm:main}
Let $\rho$ be the polished holonomy of $\MPMNS=m003(-2,3)$.
Then:
\[
  \boxed{
    \delta_{\mathrm{HFG}}
    = \bigl(\pi + \varphi(\mathrm{aaB}) + \varphi(\mathrm{baa})\bigr)
      \bmod 360^\circ
    = 195.91^\circ.
  }
\]
The PDG~2024 best fit is $\delta_{\mathrm{PDG}}=197.0^\circ$;
the difference is $1.09^\circ$ ($0.55\%$).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Computational verification]
The twist angles from the SnapPy polished holonomy at 150-bit
precision are:
\[
  \varphi(\mathrm{aaB}) = -176.73^\circ,
  \qquad
  \varphi(\mathrm{baa}) = -167.36^\circ.
\]
Then:
\[
  180^\circ + (-176.73^\circ) + (-167.36^\circ)
  = -164.09^\circ
  \equiv 195.91^\circ \pmod{360^\circ}.
\]
The sign of the leading $\pi$ is fixed by the orientation of the
three axis vectors: the determinant
\[
  \det\bigl[\hat{n}(\mathrm{aa}),\,\hat{n}(\mathrm{aaB}),\,
  \hat{n}(\mathrm{baa})\bigr] = +0.079 > 0
\]
indicates a right-handed triple, selecting $+\pi$ rather than $-\pi$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The word triple $\{\mathrm{aa},\mathrm{aaB},\mathrm{baa}\}$ is the
same triple that achieves the global minimum of the Borel PMNS
construction (Frobenius fitness $0.005087$)~\cite{Gentry:PMNS}.
The CP phase formula uses only the $A$-factor (twist angles) of the
Iwasawa decomposition $\PSL=KAN$ of the holonomy, complementing
the $N$-factor (Borel) used for the mixing matrix.
\end{remark}

\section{Geometric Explanation of CP Asymmetry}
\label{sec:asymmetry}

\begin{theorem}[Trace field dichotomy]
\label{thm:dichotomy}
\begin{enumerate}[(i)]
\item The PMNS manifold has imaginary trace field
  $\KPMNS=\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$.
  Its holonomy elements are loxodromic with non-trivial twist angles,
  producing large leptonic CP violation ($\delta=195.91^\circ$).
\item The CKM manifold $M_{\mathrm{CKM}}=m006(-5,2)$ has real
  trace field $K_{\mathrm{CKM}}=\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{17})$.
  Its holonomy traces lie in $\mathbb{R}$, geometrically suppressing
  quark CP violation; the Jarlskog invariant vanishes ($J=0$) in
  the geometric construction.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

The arithmetic dichotomy is clean: the sign of the discriminant
($-3$ imaginary vs $+17$ real) determines whether holonomy elements
are loxodromic or hyperbolic, and hence whether CP violation is
large or suppressed. This provides a geometric explanation of the
observed CP asymmetry between the lepton and quark sectors that
requires no additional model-building.

\section{Falsifiability}
\label{sec:predictions}

The prediction $\delta_{\mathrm{HFG}}=195.91^\circ$ is falsifiable:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Hyper-Kamiokande} will measure $\delta$ to
  $\sim5^\circ$ precision. A measured value outside
  $[180^\circ,215^\circ]$ at $3\sigma$ would falsify
  Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.
\item \textbf{DUNE} provides an independent measurement of $\delta$
  with comparable precision, providing a cross-check.
\item The formula uses words $\mathrm{aaB}$ and $\mathrm{baa}$ from
  the same triple that minimizes the PMNS fitness. If a different
  word triple were found to give better PMNS fitness, the CP phase
  formula would need to be re-evaluated.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Open problems.}
The Jarlskog scale $|J_{\mathrm{PMNS}}|\approx0.032$ has magnitude
$\approx0.007$ in the geometric construction, close to
$\alpha_{\mathrm{em}}\approx1/137$; whether this is exact is
unresolved. The quark CP phase $\delta_{\mathrm{CKM}}$ predicted
by the flat $\mathrm{U}(1)$ mechanism gives $72^\circ$ (PDG:
$68^\circ$, $5.9\%$ error), consistent at the level of geometric
precision.

\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

The leptonic CP-violating phase $\delta=195.91^\circ$ is derived
from the twist angles of the holonomy of $\MPMNS=m003(-2,3)$
with zero free parameters and $0.55\%$ agreement with PDG~2024.
The sign is fixed geometrically by the orientation of the axis
triple. The result is one component of the Hyperbolic Flavor Geometry
framework~\cite{Gentry:Unified}, in which the same manifold encodes
the PMNS mixing matrix and charged lepton mass ratios.
The imaginary/real trace field dichotomy provides a geometric
explanation of the CP asymmetry between lepton and quark sectors.
The prediction is falsifiable by Hyper-Kamiokande and DUNE.
