\begin{abstract}
We present a unified geometric framework in which the Standard Model
flavor parameters---CKM and PMNS mixing matrices, the leptonic
CP-violating phase, and the charged fermion mass ratios---are encoded
in the arithmetic of two compact hyperbolic 3-manifolds:
$\MPMNS = m003(-2,3)$ and $\MCKM = m006(-5,2)$,
both with $H_1 = \Zfive$ and volume below $2.1$.
The PMNS mixing matrix arises from the Borel factor of the holonomy
representation of $\MPMNS$ (Frobenius fitness $0.005087$ to PDG~2024---
the global minimum of the construction).
The leptonic CP-violating phase is derived parameter-freely:
$\delta_{\mathrm{HFG}} = \pi + \varphi(\mathrm{aaB}) + \varphi(\mathrm{baa})
= 195.91^\circ$ ($0.55\%$ from PDG).
The trace fields $\KPMNS = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$ (imaginary) and
$\KCKM = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{17})$ (real) explain the CP asymmetry
between sectors geometrically.
All charged fermion mass ratios are approximated by norms in
$\Zom$ (leptons: $\tau$ error $0.006\%$) and $\Zsev$
(quarks: $b$ error $0.012\%$, $t$ error $0.0065\%$),
with permutation null-test $p<0.002$.
The framework is falsifiable by Hyper-Kamiokande, DUNE, and CMB-S4.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

The flavor parameters of the Standard Model---the CKM~\cite{Kobayashi}
and PMNS~\cite{Maki} mixing matrices, CP-violating phases, and fermion
mass ratios---have no derivation from first principles.
We show they are consistent with encoding in two compact arithmetic
hyperbolic 3-manifolds of minimal volume with $H_1 = \Zfive$.
The construction has four independently testable layers:
(i)~mixing matrices from Iwasawa decomposition of holonomy;
(ii)~leptonic CP phase from twist angles, parameter-free;
(iii)~CP asymmetry from the imaginary/real trace field dichotomy;
(iv)~fermion mass ratios as norms in the rings of integers of the trace fields.

A direct test shows that primitive geodesic lengths do \emph{not}
statistically encode the fermion mass lattice (enrichment factor $0.40$,
i.e.\ depletion). The arithmetic encoding operates at a deeper level
through the ring-of-integers norm forms.

\subsection{Selection of $\MPMNS$}

Among all closed orientable hyperbolic 3-manifolds with
$H_1=\Zfive$ in the first 500 census entries, $\MPMNS$ is the unique
element satisfying:
(a)~minimal volume ($0.9814$, smallest known~\cite{GMM});
(b)~Lucas-pure covering tower: all new torsion primes through degree~9
are prime Lucas numbers $\{2,3,11\}$, and $13$ never appears;
(c)~a geodesic of length $\frac{3}{2}\log\vp=0.72157$ (second shortest,
to $0.03\%$)---the smearing parameter that minimises the CKM construction.

\subsection{Trace fields}

\begin{proposition}
$\KPMNS = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$ and $\KCKM = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{17})$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Verification]
For $\MPMNS$: $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$ is established for the cusped
ancestor $m003$ in~\cite{Chinburg98}.
For $\MCKM$: $\mathrm{tr}(\rho(aa))=3-\sqrt{17}$, satisfying
$x^2-6x-8=0$ with discriminant $68=4\cdot17$, verified at 150-bit
precision. Rings of integers: $\Zom$ (norm $a^2-ab+b^2$) and
$\Zsev$ (norm $|a^2-17b^2|$).
\end{proof}

$p<10^{-4}$ vs $50{,}000$ Haar-random unitary matrices.

\section{Geometric CP Phase}
\label{sec:cp}

\begin{theorem}[CP phase, zero free parameters]
\label{thm:cp}
\[
  \delta_{\mathrm{HFG}}
  = \pi + \varphi(\mathrm{aaB}) + \varphi(\mathrm{baa})
  = 195.91^\circ,
\]
where $\varphi(\gamma)=\mathrm{Im}\log\lambda(\gamma)$.
PDG 2024: $197.0^\circ$; difference $1.09^\circ$ ($0.55\%$).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Verification]
$\varphi(\mathrm{aaB})=-176.73^\circ$,
$\varphi(\mathrm{baa})=-167.36^\circ$ from 150-bit polished holonomy.
Sign fixed by $\det[\hat n(\mathrm{aa}),\hat n(\mathrm{aaB}),
\hat n(\mathrm{baa})]=+0.079>0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[CP asymmetry from trace fields]
\label{thm:cp_asym}
$\KPMNS$ is imaginary $\Rightarrow$ loxodromic holonomy $\Rightarrow$
large leptonic CP.
$\KCKM$ is real $\Rightarrow$ holonomy traces in $\mathbb{R}$
$\Rightarrow$ geometric CP suppression ($J_\mathrm{CKM}=0$).
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}
The Lucas primes $L_{11}=199=N(-15-13\omega)$ and
$L_{17}=3571=N(-69-34\omega)$ are also Eisenstein norms (exact),
with errors $3.8\%$ and $2.7\%$; they satisfy $\equiv1\pmod3$
(split in $\KPMNS$) and $\equiv3\pmod4$ (not Gaussian norms),
arithmetically locking them to $\KPMNS$.
The witnesses in the table are the nearest integer norms within
search radius $R=150$; for the muon, $N=208$ is closer to $m_\mu/m_e$
than the Lucas prime $L_{11}=199$ ($0.59\%$ vs $3.8\%$).
The Lucas prime is included in the remark because of its additional
arithmetic significance (prime, split, exact Eisenstein norm),
not because of superior accuracy.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Statistical validation}

A permutation null test ($N=500$ shuffles of mass assignments) gives
$p<0.002$ for $\Zsev$ on mean log-error (observed $-2.605$,
null mean $-2.098$) and on fraction within $2\%$ (observed $0.75$,
null mean $0.62$).
Comparison against 10 random real quadratic discriminants shows
$d=37$ achieves marginally better mean log-error ($-2.618$ vs
$-2.605$); the identification $d=17$ rests on the independent
arithmetic $\mathrm{tr}(\rho(aa))=3-\sqrt{17}$, not on error
minimization alone.

\section{Covering Tower and Lucas-Purity}
\label{sec:covering}

\begin{observation}
Through degree~9, every new torsion prime in finite covers of
$\MPMNS$ is a prime Lucas number from $\{2,3,11\}$;
the prime $13$ never appears.
\end{observation}

\begin{conjecture}[Lucas-purity]
All torsion primes in the covering tower of $\MPMNS$ are prime Lucas numbers.
Proof would require connecting torsion growth to Hecke eigenvalues
of the quaternion algebra of discriminant~3 via Jacquet-Langlands.
\end{conjecture}

\section{Predictions and Open Problems}
\label{sec:predictions}

\paragraph{Falsifiable predictions.}
(1)~$\delta_{\mathrm{PMNS}} = 195.91^\circ$, testable by
Hyper-Kamiokande and DUNE ($\sim5^\circ$ precision).
(2)~The prime~13 never appears in any finite cover of $\MPMNS$.
(3)~Neutrino masses (speculative, no direct geometric support):
$\sum m_\nu\approx71$~meV, testable by CMB-S4.

\paragraph{Open problems.}
(1)~Derive $m_e$ from geometric invariants (volume, CS$=1/4$,
systole).
(2)~Explain the Jarlskog scale $\approx0.007$; proximity to
$\alpha_{\mathrm{em}}\approx1/137$ may or may not be exact.
(3)~Compute Bianchi modular form Hecke eigenvalues over
$\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3})$ (requires MAGMA); test whether they are
Lucas numbers.
(4)~Extend covering tower to degree~12 and beyond.
(5)~Dirac eigenvalue computation (requires specialized hyperbolic
PDE solver).
(6)~Gauge structure: embed $\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{SU}(2)
\times\mathrm{U}(1)$ in the construction.

\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

We have shown that two arithmetic hyperbolic 3-manifolds encode the
CKM and PMNS mixing matrices, the leptonic CP phase (zero free
parameters, $0.55\%$ error), and all charged fermion mass ratios
(permutation $p<0.002$). The CP asymmetry between sectors is
explained by the imaginary/real trace field dichotomy.
The framework is a geometric phenomenology analogous to the Balmer
formula: the pattern is established; the dynamical foundation
awaits discovery.
