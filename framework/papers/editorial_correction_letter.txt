Dear Editors,

We are writing to submit a correction to our manuscripts RINP-D-26-00327 
(CKM) and RINP-D-26-00328 (PMNS) currently under review at Results in Physics.

During post-submission verification, we identified an error in the PMNS 
target matrix used in the fitness computation of RINP-D-26-00328. The 
matrix employed in the original submission was not consistent with the 
standard PDG parametrization of the PMNS unitary matrix. We have corrected 
this to use the PDG 2024 values:

  theta_12 = 33.65 deg (sin^2 theta_12 = 0.307)
  theta_23 = 47.64 deg (sin^2 theta_23 = 0.546)  
  theta_13 = 8.57  deg (sin^2 theta_13 = 0.02219)
  delta_CP = 197 deg

The corrected computation yields an improved result: fitness = 0.005087 
(vs. 0.013785 in the original submission), with mixing angles:

  theta_12 = 33.7 deg  (PDG: 33.65 deg, diff 0.05 deg)
  theta_23 = 47.6 deg  (PDG: 47.64 deg, diff 0.04 deg)
  theta_13 = 8.4  deg  (PDG: 8.57  deg, diff 0.17 deg)

This result is stronger than originally reported. The corrected fitness 
is 2.7x better than the published value, and all three mixing angles now 
agree with PDG 2024 to within 0.2 degrees.

For RINP-D-26-00327 (CKM), the target matrix used PDG values from an 
earlier year (max difference from PDG 2024: 0.001). The CKM fitness value 
of 0.017290 is unchanged under the updated PDG values.

We attach a corrected version of RINP-D-26-00328 with the updated fitness 
value, mixing angle table, and corrected target matrix specification. We 
apologize for the error and appreciate the opportunity to submit a corrected 
version.
